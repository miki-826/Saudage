## コンビニの記憶

ツール: `image_gen.imagegen` built-in。生成結果1536×1024 PNG。配信用は横1200px、WebP quality 86。

実プロンプト:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: memory reveal illustration for a Japanese atmospheric narrative web game called LIVE. A quiet small Japanese convenience store at night seen from inside, warmly glowing amber shelves, a cardboard box of groceries on the foreground counter, wet windows reflecting a streetlamp, a small paper coffee cup, cinematic painterly anime background art, sophisticated detailed lighting, dark navy shadows and warm antique gold highlights, nostalgic intimate feeling of forgotten everyday memories. Wide landscape 1536x1024 composition. No people, no text, no readable labels, no logo, no interface. This is an illustration of a remembered place. Rich natural textures, restrained warm palette.

文字や操作要素のない情景として生成。夜の店・品出し・接客・常連客の記憶でクロップ位置を変えて利用。実際の記憶解放画面で表示確認。画像から商品の事実や店名を定義しない。

## キャラクター

ツール: `image_gen.imagegen` built-in。生成結果1254×1254 PNG。配信用は横1200px、WebP quality 86。

実プロンプト:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: character portrait for atmospheric Japanese narrative game LIVE. A young adult Japanese woman age 24 with a short softly layered dark brown bob haircut and brown eyes, wearing an understated charcoal cardigan over a cream blouse. Head and shoulders portrait, facing the viewer with a quiet pensive expression, a slight emerging warmth, introspective and natural. Cinematic painterly anime illustration, refined semi-realistic rendering, finely painted strands of hair. Rainy old street softly blurred behind her, warm amber streetlight from the right and deep navy night shadows. Rich antique gold and near-black palette, understated emotional beauty. Square composition. No text, no UI, no logos, no jewelry emphasis.

提供のUI参考画像に合わせた新規キャラクター。元画像の切り抜きではない。会話画面・タイトル小画像・エンディングに使用。進捗に応じCSSで彩度と明るさを変える。表情別の複数画像やリップシンクは未実装。PC・モバイルのクロップを確認。
